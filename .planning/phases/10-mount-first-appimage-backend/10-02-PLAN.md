<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| wrapper process to OS signals | SIGINT/SIGTERM/SIGHUP arrive from the user, the terminal, or the client host; they must not orphan children in separate process groups. |
| helper lifetime to mount lifetime | The helper can die or the mount can vanish while the app-server still runs; the supervisor must not treat the stale root as valid. |
| helper output to error/log text | Failure text is untrusted and may be unbounded or newline-forged. |
