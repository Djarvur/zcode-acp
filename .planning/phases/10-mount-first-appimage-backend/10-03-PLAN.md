<objective>
Replace the two order-coupled AppImage probes and the extraction seam with one pure-read AppImage-layout stage: collect packed files, unpacked trees, and an XDG AppImage-family seed; evaluate each candidate individually; rank by version; apply the documented tie-break; and return at most one winner with its layout, version, and resolved path (DISC-01, DISC-02, DISC-03, DISC-04, MNT-06; D-06, D-07, D-08, D-09, D-10, D-12, D-13). VER-01 is owned by the fake-runtime mount tests in 10-01/10-02, not by this discovery plan.

Purpose: close the defect that lets a stale unpacked 3.18.2 tree pin an older desktop while a current 4.0.0 packed AppImage sits beside it, and make one bad candidate (the real machine's dangling `ZCode.AppImage` symlink) a diagnostic instead of a whole-probe abort. Discovery remains side-effect-free: mounting happens only after selection, at acquisition time (D-12).

Output: the version-ranked candidate stage, packed `Install` values, the `appimage-rank` and `appimage-candidate` diagnostics, XDG seeding, the deferred bundled-builtin row for packed winners, and deletion of `Probers.ExtractAppImage`/`defaultExtractAppImage`.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| filesystem scan to candidate list | ~/Applications entries, symlinks, and the XDG entry target are untrusted path shapes. |
| candidate list to Install | A candidate's resolved path becomes the AppImage executed later; it must be a regular executable, not a decoy or dangling link. |
| diagnostics to logs | Paths and miss reasons are user-controlled strings rendered into logs and errors. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-11 | Spoofing | symlink/path traversal in candidates | high | mitigate | EvalSymlinks per candidate, regular-file and executable-bit checks, and a resolved-path dedupe; a failure is a miss, never an adopted root. |
| T-10-12 | Tampering | volatile mount adopted as spawn root | high | mitigate | mountDecoy rejects /tmp/.mount_* for discovered and user-supplied paths; the wrapper's own managed mount is created only after selection and is never discovered. |
| T-10-13 | Denial of service | one bad candidate aborting discovery | medium | mitigate | Per-candidate evaluation with miss rows; iteration always continues; the winner is chosen from the surviving hits. |
| T-10-14 | Tampering | TOCTOU replacement between discovery and exec | low | accept | Resolve once and exec the resolved path; the mount is read-only; the path is user-controlled and signature verification is out of scope (must not be claimed). |
| T-10-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | No package installs; `mise run tidy-diff` enforces the existing single require line. |
</threat_model>
