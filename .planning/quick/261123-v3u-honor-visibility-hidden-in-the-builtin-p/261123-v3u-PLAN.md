<objective>
Honor the builtin provider table's `config.visibility: "hidden"` marker in the account overlay: `BuildAccount` and the snapshot-derived read-back want list must never include hidden zhipu-account rules, so the idle/highspeed account providers stop appearing as duplicate rows in the model picker catalog.

Purpose: The desktop's own model-selection view filters `config.visibility !== "hidden"`; the wrapper drops the field and pushes every zhipu-account rule as `entitled: true, current: true`, which materializes the hidden idle/highspeed providers in the registry and consequently in `settings.model.available` — the catalog the wrapper mirrors verbatim (D-08). Omitting a table-declared hidden provider at snapshot-build time is the fix; there is no wrapper-side catalog filtering.

Output: A visibility-aware `BuildAccount`, updated doc comments, and RED/GREEN test locks at the snapshot tier and the want-list tier.

Non-goals (explicit): do NOT filter visible rules (individual/team/start); do NOT touch the ACP configOptions translation, `mergeCatalogEntries`, or add any client-side filtering (that re-opens the catalog-shrink fix 817f83e/91e86f3).
</objective>
