<objective>
Preserve an established ACP session when `session/setModel` rejects a recoverable model-selection validation error, while retaining full teardown for genuinely fatal or dead-target failures. Add regression coverage for the observed unsupported-reasoning-effort refusal, a later valid operation, the `/model` path, and fatal-error teardown.

Purpose: the confirmed Orca trace records `session/setModel` failing with `-32603` because reasoning effort `"max"` is unsupported, followed 31 seconds later by `session/set_config_option` failing because the wrapper has already dropped that session's confirmed settings. The current unconditional teardown turns a correctable selection refusal into a dead ACP session.

Output: one shared, conservative error classifier; both setModel refusal paths gated through it; regression tests proving session/settings preservation and retry, plus unambiguous fatal teardown coverage.
</objective>

Classification decision: use structured numeric/error codes first. Treat `-32602` as recoverable. Do not broaden this to all internal errors and do not classify resource-not-found, method-not-found, connection-closed, cancellation, malformed data, or unknown data codes as recoverable.
